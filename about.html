Get to know me

Strategic by design, restless by nature

I see life as a journey meant to be explored, not rushed through. I believe in collecting experiences from different places, ideas and challenges, because each one teaches something valuable. Growth comes from staying curious and showing up every day with the intention to do better than yesterday.

I try to live a "versatile life" — I don't want my days to feel repetitive or limited. I like stepping into new roles, facing unfamiliar challenges, and learning through real effort. The motto that keeps me grounded is "Mess with the best, rest like death." It isn't about competing with others — it's a promise to myself to take my work seriously, respect excellence, and never be careless with my potential.
