About

I am MD. Tajwar Hasan Ishmam.

A Bangladeshi law student, filmmaker, content creator and orator based in Chattogram — currently pursuing LL.B. (Honours) at International Islamic University Chittagong (IIUC).

I move between the courtroom's discipline and the camera's patience. Law taught me to think in structure; cinema taught me to notice. Both, for me, are ways of paying attention to people.
